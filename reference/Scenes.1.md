## Cast

**Dr. Nālani Akana**, 44. Harvard Undergrad, Scripps Ph.D. Married at Scripps, Two
children while grad student.  Widowed. Ocean acoustician, University of Hawaiʻi at Hilo. PhD at Scripps on cryogenic signals in IMS hydroacoustic data — she wrote her dissertation on iceberg tremor recorded at Cape Leeuwin. Turned down a SOEST position at Mānoa and a NOAA slot at PMEL to come home to Hilo: a 3-3 teaching load, two hydrophones maintained on borrowed ship time, and a cron job she wrote years ago that watches the open IMS feed because nobody would fund a person to do it. Gives the annual tsunami-awareness talk at the Hilo library. Her grandmother, **Tūtū Ruth**, 91, survived the 1946 wave as a child; the family store on Kamehameha Avenue went in 1960 and was never rebuilt — it is parkland now, and Nālani can stand on the grass where the register sat. The book does not explain this or apologize for it.

**Daniel Grier**, 51. Deputy director, NOAA Tsunami Program, Silver Spring. Oceanography PhD, then four years as a postdoc at Scripps — overlapping Nālani's PhD, in the same building, and he knew her husband as well as he knew her. His own science never landed: two papers that went nowhere, a grant declined twice, a second postdoc as the only offer on the table. He chose the government instead and has never called it a defeat out loud. Six years as a PTWC duty scientist at Ewa Beach in his thirties — the 24/7 rotation, the seismic screen, the fifteen minutes between an earthquake and a warning — then Silver Spring and the slow climb to deputy director. Nine years ago he sat on the panel that scored her array proposal out of funding; the review said "insufficient institutional leverage," which meant she wouldn't move to Mānoa. He remembers writing it, and she has had his cell number the whole time. Competent, careful, institutionally fluent, and aware that fluency is what he traded ambition for.

**Ellen Grier**, 49. Health-policy lobbyist, K Street. Married Daniel at the end of the Scripps years, when he still looked like a scientist with a future; Maya was born during the Ewa Beach posting. **The marriage is good** — not a truce, not an arrangement, good — and the one thing in it that does not sit right is his ambition, or the absence of it. She has floated three jobs past him in a decade (a consultancy, an association directorship, a policy shop) and he has declined all three without ever quite arguing. Her worry is specific and unspoken: in this city a spouse is part of a portfolio, and she is being read as a woman without one behind her. Her family's cottage has stood on the Currituck Banks for four generations. The argument about whether to repair or sell it is a real argument about a real house, and nothing else — which is why losing it costs them something instead of proving something.

**Maya Grier**, 16. Their daughter. Present, watchful, keeps score.

**E. Sandoval**, AFTAC duty analyst, Patrick SFB. Screens the Day 0 hydroacoustic signals as non-nuclear — correctly, immediately — and files them. Appears twice. The second time is unbearable, because the first time was right.

**Supporting, as needed:** Hilo's civil defense administrator (**Kawika Pang**), who trusts Nālani because she has shown up for eleven straight years; Daniel's boss, an SES careerist who owns the word "premature"; a Perth colleague of Nālani's on the HA01 side of the ocean.

## Narrative mode

Alternate close-third scenes (the two POVs below) with the found documents already drafted in Fourteen Days. The documents carry the planet; the scenes carry the two people. A reader should sometimes encounter the document first and the scene behind it a chapter later — the Bulletin 003 cancellation, then Daniel drafting his concurrence to it. The gap between what the record says and what the room felt is the book's texture.

# PART ONE — THE DAY

### S1 · D−1, evening · Hilo · Nālani
**Drafted in `lyx/ice.lyx`; both sketches superseded. See `Scenes.2.md` S1.** In outline: the catechism at the table, Lehua reciting 1946 and Waiākea back at her mother; Bzzzz as the book's first section break; Oscar and the typed investigation behind a closed door; HA01 railed, Crozet (HA04) confirming, a hyperbola through Prydz Bay; the four Prydz shore stations silent at the origin instant and dismissed; and Tūtū's *"How long."*
*Function: establish her expertise, her community, and the family memory in one room. The book's first believer is a woman whose grandmother already knows what the ocean does.*

### S2 · D0, 7:00 am EDT · Silver Spring · Daniel
Breakfast first: the Corolla cottage — repair estimate, flood insurance non-renewal, her refusal to sell, his refusal to say what he thinks. Two people who like each other disagreeing about money, with Maya eating standing up because it is faster, not because there is anything to stay out of range of. On her way out Ellen mentions a directorship that is opening, lightly, the fourth time in ten years; he says he'll look at it and they both know he won't; she kisses him anyway. Then the office, where four notices from four agencies sit in his queue, and the desk consensus assembles itself: floating ice, Larsen B precedent, displaces its own weight, no seismic source, no wave. Every sentence true. He concurs.
*Function: plant the D+8 payoff; put Daniel on the record agreeing with the reasoning that becomes Bulletin 003. His error is not negligence. That is the point.*

### S3 · D0, mid-morning EDT · phone · both
Nālani does not go through the voicemail trees; she has had his cell number for twenty years. That is the scene — not a stranger reached at last, but an old friend called at 4 am his time, by a woman who knew him when he was still going to be a scientist. Neither of them has to say *you reviewed my array proposal*, so neither does, and it sits in the call the whole way through. She makes the four-pins case: four sectors, one day, a circumpolar sequence, and then the thing nobody has a desk for — the trough-mouth fans, loaded to the angle of repose, pore pressure re-equilibrating on a clock of hours. "I'm not calling about the ice. I'm calling about the mud." He gives her the textbook, correctly. After the call he writes *pore pressure?* on a legal pad and does nothing with it, and the doing-nothing is a scene, not a summary.
*Function: first contact, pre-loaded with grievance. Her science is right and unfundable; his caution is wrong and institutional. Neither is a villain.*

### S4 · D0, afternoon · Patrick SFB, then Silver Spring · document + Daniel
Sandoval's AFTAC screening note, presented as a document: signal non-nuclear, correctly discriminated, no further action — the treaty machine working exactly as designed, and therefore telling no one. Then Daniel in the ops room as the Indian Ocean models converge on sub-decimetre amplitudes and the cancellation goes out. He drafts the US concurrence himself. The chapter ends on his signature.
*Function: every gate fails open and nobody makes a mistake. The reader watches the trap close in real time.*

# PART TWO — THE MUD

### S5 · D0, night EDT · Silver Spring ops floor · Daniel
The Prydz fan lets go eleven hours after the ice. Daniel is in the building on an unrelated pretext when the Arabian Sea gauges start posting. Jamnagar. Karachi. The room does not erupt; it goes quiet, which is worse. Someone prints his concurrence memo without being asked to, and he understands it has become an exhibit. He calls the only person who was right. It is dinnertime in Hilo. She answers on the first ring, and she does not say I told you so, because she is already on the next problem.
*Function: the false all-clear detonates. Their working alliance begins in the worst possible hour of his career.*

### S6 · D0 evening HST → D1 pre-dawn · Hilo · Nālani
She runs the Ross clock on a whiteboard in her lab with two undergrads: fan failure plus transit equals Hilo before dawn, and Ewa Beach an hour behind it — PTWC gets its warning from a woman its parent agency declined to fund, about a wave that will reach the warning center's own parking lot. Then the part her whole life has been rehearsal for: Kawika Pang takes her call at 9 pm because it is her, and the evacuation runs down the routes she has walked with library audiences for eleven years. She puts Tūtū Ruth in the car herself. Her grandmother directs the packing: photographs, the koa bowl, nothing else. "We've done this."
*Function: the community work pays off as infrastructure. Memory is the only warning system that fires.*

### S7 · D1, 2:40 am HST · Hilo · Nālani
The Hilo harbor event. Written to Hawaii.md: the bay finds its note and rings — not one wave but a seiche, building for hours, the harbor destroying itself in the dark by increments. The water crosses the breakwater, takes the port, floods the bayfront parkland, and stops, roughly, at the grass line where the town already retreated — where the 1960 dead ended and the survivors chose not to rebuild. Dawn scene: Nālani stands at the edge of the flooded park on the spot where the family store stood, ocean on one side, living town on the other, and understands that her grandmother's generation drew this line and the line held. Casualties in single digits. The harbor is gone; Hilo is not.
*Function: the book's moral center. One American town treated memory as policy, sixty years early, and the water honored the treaty.*

### S8 · D1, ~1 pm EDT · Washington/Annapolis · Daniel
The Crary wave, per EastCoast.md: two to four metres at the open coast, Sandy-comparison, Norfolk flooded, the Chesapeake's bent shallow water stripping the wave before it reaches DC. Daniel's own house is dry and he finds the dryness obscurely shameful. Maya is at a friend's in Annapolis — real fear, brief peril, resolved in an hour; use it lightly. Ellen's first question, before Maya is even confirmed safe: "What about Corolla?" The cottage takes water and stands. That is the trap: it stands.
*Function: the East Coast gets its inconclusive wave. The cottage surviving Day 1 makes D+8 personal instead of merely regional, and buys a week of false relief inside the marriage.*

# PART THREE — THE WEEK

### S9 · D+3–D+5 · Silver Spring / Hilo, by phone · both
Norway goes on D+3 and the pattern stops being deniable: margins failing on their own clocks, each loaded by its own vanished neighbour. Daniel converts his disgrace into a mandate — a fan-watch task force — and brings Nālani in as its scientific anchor, by phone, unpaid at first, because the paperwork moves slower than the mud. The 3 am calls acquire a rhythm. They talk pore pressure and then keep talking. Ellen notices the cadence of the calls rather than their content and is not jealous, because the marriage is good and because she has met Nālani twice. What she notices is that at 3 am her husband sounds like a man who wants something, and that in twenty years she has never once heard him sound that way about a job she found him.
*Function: the relationship tension starts here, and it starts as the intimacy of being the only two people who trust each other's numbers. Nālani values precisely the carefulness Ellen reads as timidity — the same man, two accurate readings. See Dials.*

### S10 · D+6–D+7 · Silver Spring · Daniel
The Currituck question. The slide has been mapped since the 1980s; it has failed before; and Nālani's argument — the Crary wave's passage swung bottom pressure across the whole Virginia margin, tens of kilopascals on a primed fan — says the clock is running. No agency owns the decision to evacuate a coastline on a pore-pressure model. Daniel forces it: the same institutional gate that failed open on Day 0 is now his to hold shut, in public, against his boss's "premature," with his name on it either way. A partial evacuation order goes out for the Outer Banks and lower Chesapeake. Ellen drives to Corolla anyway — to empty the cottage, not in defiance of him but because she has spent a week watching him be careful in public and does not experience her own husband as an authority to be obeyed. She thinks there is time. He would have said there was time too, if she had asked, and she did not ask, and that is the whole failure. Maya goes with her. He finds out from Maya's text.
*Function: his redemption arc and his marriage crisis are the same decision. He can warn a coast and cannot persuade his wife.*

### S11 · D+8 · Currituck Banks / Silver Spring · both households
The slide goes mid-afternoon. Warning is measured in hours from the precursor Nālani flagged, then in minutes once the margin fails — forty minutes from failure to shore. Ellen and Maya are on the causeway inland with the koa-bowl equivalent of four generations in the back seat because his evacuation order emptied the roads a day early; the order he could not make her believe is the reason she lives. The cottage goes entirely — not flooded, gone, the lot itself resurveyed by the ocean. Aftermath scene that night, all three Griers in the Silver Spring kitchen: grief for the house arriving as grief for the marriage's imagined future. Write the scene so it can break either way. See Dials.
*Function: D+8 lands where you asked it to — inside the Washington household. The catastrophe and the marriage stop being separate plots.*

### S12 · D+12 · split scene, phone · both — PART THREE CODA
The hinge, verbatim from Sequence.md: gauges are back in service and none of them return to predicted level. Wave residual decays; this does not. Nālani on the lava shore at night, Daniel in the dark ops room, the same residual on both screens. One of them says: "It's not going back down." The disaster — the shape with an end on it — is over. The load has begun.
*Function: pivot the book from event to condition. If the novel ends at Part Three, this is the final chapter and it ends on that line.*

# PART FOUR — THE LOAD (optional, sketch only)

Time-jump structure, chapters years apart, per Decades.md. The staircase: pinning-point stalls and surges, each stall producing the argument that it's over, each surge the argument that it was always understated. The fingerprint delivers the North Atlantic its maximum — the hemisphere that watched Day 1 on television inherits the largest permanent rise. Threshold failures end cities that no wave ever reached: insurance withdrawal, the drains running backward, Norfolk. Daniel leaves government after the second stall makes his task force look alarmist; testifies when the surge makes it look prophetic; grows old inside that oscillation. Nālani becomes, against her entire temperament, the national witness for retreat — because Hilo is the only American precedent, and she is its literate survivor. The Oahu flank question (Hawaii.md §3, the Nuuanu-type failure that beams at the mainland) hangs over the decades as the book's loaded gun; fire it late or leave it forever cocked — see Dials. Closing image at stabilization, Y60+: Nālani in her nineties, or her student, standing at Hilo's third shoreline park — the town that retreated twice and is still there — with the register-spot of the family store now some distance out under clear water.

## Dials — decisions that are yours, with my recommendation

**1. The affair.** Options: (a) consummated during the fortnight; (b) unconsummated — the pull is on the page, acknowledged once, acted on never; (c) deferred — nothing during the crisis, open in Part Four after the marriage resolves. I recommend (b) for Parts One–Three, with (c) available if Part Four exists and the marriage breaks. Reasoning: if they sleep together in week two, D+11's kitchen scene becomes about her, and the marriage crisis stops being about the marriage. The stronger version keeps Ellen's grievance accurate but incomplete — the calls really are just pore pressure, and they really are also not.

**2. The marriage.** Reframed: the marriage is good, so nothing breaks it by attrition. If it breaks it must break on the ambition question — Ellen finally understanding that he will never want what she wants, which is a legitimate and non-villainous reason to leave a man you love. The D+8 aftermath can start that (the empty lot, and his refusal to convert being right into anything) or settle it (the lot clears the only argument they had). Both are honest. If Part Four exists, the break lands better at Y6–Y9, when he is first notorious and then prophetic and takes nothing from either.

**3. The ending point.** Three clean exits: the Hilo dawn (S7, a novella), the hinge (S12, the full disaster novel), or stabilization (Part Four, the condition novel). The material supports all three; only the third uses the Oahu gun.

**4. Sandoval.** Keep as the AFTAC cameo (two documents, no scenes) or cut entirely and fold the screening note into S4 narration. I'd keep the cameo: the machine that was right needs one face.

**5. Names.** All placeholders. If you keep a Native Hawaiian identity for Nālani, at manuscript stage a cultural read of the Hilo and family material is worth the cost; the alternative — a long-term transplant who chose Hilo — loses the store-in-the-parkland inheritance, which I would fight to keep.

## Consistency ledger (internal bookkeeping only)

4. The cottage survives D1 and dies D+8. Nothing in between may damage it.
