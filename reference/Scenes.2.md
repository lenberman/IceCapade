## Cast

**Dr. Nālani Akana**, 44. Harvard undergrad, Scripps PhD on cryogenic signals in IMS
hydroacoustic data — iceberg tremor recorded at HA01 Cape Leeuwin. Married at Scripps to
**Tomás Reyes**; two children born while she was still a graduate student. At completion she
turned down a SOEST position at Mānoa and a NOAA slot at PMEL in Newport, Oregon, and came home
to Hilo — **a decision she made married, at 29, with her husband agreeing to it and giving up
his own better options to do so.** The household is the point: she moved in with Tūtū Ruth, and
it has been four people in one house ever since.

What Hilo gets her: a 3-3 teaching load, two hydrophones maintained on borrowed ship time, the annual tsunami-awareness talk at the library, and **Oscar** — an LLM assistant she set up years ago, when her grant proposals stopped being funded, and pointed at the hydroacoustic feeds to watch for ice. Four alerts in six years. Many people watch those feeds; few watch for ice. See Ledger #20. What it costs her is the whole first act.

The reason she came home is on the page in S1 and it is Tūtū's tales. Ledger #8's order still governs — the decision is joint, made at 29 with Tomás alive and agreeing — but the *motive* the book supplies is inherited memory.

Tomás died six years before Day 0 — ordinary, slow, eighteen months of it. She has not
remarried and the book does not stage this as a question. His model code is still on her
machine, still commented in his hand, and she still runs it. **The book does not explain this
or apologize for it.**

**Tūtū Ruth**, 91. Nālani's grandmother; survived the 1946 wave as a child. The family store on
Kamehameha Avenue went in 1960 and was never rebuilt — it is parkland now, and Nālani can stand
on the grass where the register sat. Note on usage: *tūtū* is Hawaiian for grandmother, not a
name. Inside the house she is **Tūtū**; the narration and outsiders say Tūtū Ruth. Locals
unrelated to her may call her Tūtū too, which is one cheap way to show how embedded the
household is.

**Ikaika Reyes**, 19. Nālani's elder. Away at university on the mainland at Day 0 — a voice on
the phone during the worst night, unable to get home, which rhymes against the choice his
mother made at his age and will not be pointed at.

**Lehua Reyes**, 16. Nālani's younger, at Hilo High, in the house. At the S1 table, in the car
in S8, on the shore at S9. Sixteen, exactly as Maya Grier is sixteen: two girls in two
households, one of whom will lose a house and one of whom will lose nothing, and neither of
whom chose either.

Her silence in S1 is directed. She withholds domestic compliance from her mother and gives her the whole catechism in the same scene. She is not withdrawn; she is sixteen and selective. Any later scene that reads her as generally uncommunicative gets it wrong.

**Daniel Grier**, 51. Deputy director, NOAA Tsunami Program, Silver Spring. Oceanography PhD,
then four years as a postdoc at Scripps, overlapping Nālani's doctorate in the same building.
He knew Tomás as well as he knew her. **His own science never landed** — two papers that went
nowhere, a grant declined twice, and a second postdoc as the only thing on the table — and he
took the government instead and has never once called that a defeat out loud.

Six years as a **PTWC duty scientist at Ewa Beach** in his thirties: the 24/7 rotation, the
seismic screen, the fifteen minutes between an earthquake somewhere and a warning to a
coastline. Maya was born during that posting. Then Silver Spring, and eleven years of climbing
carefully. Nine years ago he sat on the panel that scored Nālani's array proposal out of
funding; the review said "insufficient institutional leverage," which meant she wouldn't move
to Mānoa. He remembers writing it, and she has had his cell number the whole time.

Competent, careful, institutionally fluent, and aware that fluency is what he traded ambition
for. **Whether he went to Tomás's funeral is a live question — see Dial 8.**

**Ellen Grier**, 49. Health-policy lobbyist, K Street. Married Daniel at the end of the Scripps
years, when he still looked like a scientist with a future. **The marriage is good.** Not a
truce, not an arrangement — they like each other, they are kind to each other, and the sex and
the jokes are both still there.

The one thing in it that does not sit right is his ambition, or the absence of it. She has
floated three jobs past him in a decade — a consultancy, an association directorship, a policy
shop — and he has declined all three without ever quite arguing. Her worry is specific and
almost never spoken: in this city a spouse is part of a portfolio, and she is read as a woman
without one behind her. She is not ashamed of him. She is tired of explaining him.

Her family's cottage has stood on the Currituck Banks for four generations. The argument about
whether to repair or sell it is a real argument about a real house and nothing else — which is
why losing it costs them something instead of proving something.

**Maya Grier**, 16. Their daughter, born on Oahu, no memory of it. Present, watchful, keeps
score. POV from S20 onward.

**Keala Fonoti**, 20. Undergrad at the S7 whiteboard; Hilo-raised, attended Nālani's library
talks as a schoolchild, which is why she is in the lab at 9 pm on a Sunday. Becomes Nālani's
graduate student, then her successor in the lab.

**Marcus Yee**, 55. Curtin University, Perth — the HA01 side of the ocean. Nālani's
longest-running colleague and, in Part Four, her designated skeptic: he doubts the Lanai
deposits and doubts the creep correlation, correctly by the literature, in the two disputes
Hawaii.md §5 says must not resolve.

**E. Sandoval**, AFTAC duty analyst, Patrick SFB. Screens the Day 0 hydroacoustic signals as
non-nuclear — correctly, immediately — and files them. Two documents, no scenes. The second one
is unbearable because the first one was right.

**Oscar.** Nālani's LLM assistant, on her own machine since well before Day 0. **"It," never "he."** She named it and has no illusions about it; the book has exactly one machine with a masculine pronoun and that is Tomás's model code. **Interaction is typed, not spoken** — monospaced commands on the page. Instrument, never oracle: it fetches, computes and reports, and never interprets. It must never notice something before a human does, and it does not run Tomás's model (Ledger #15).

**Supporting:** **Kawika Pang**, Hilo civil defense, who trusts Nālani because she has shown up
for eleven straight years; Daniel's boss, an SES careerist who owns the word "premature."

## The triangle, stated plainly

Worth keeping visible while drafting, because the good marriage makes it harder and better.

Ellen finds Daniel's carefulness insufficient. Nālani finds the same carefulness to be the
whole of his integrity. **Both readings are accurate.** The pull is therefore not compensation
for a bad marriage — there isn't one — but a genuine competing account of who he is, arriving
in the only fortnight of his life when the careful reading is also the correct one.

And it runs the other way too. Nālani's marriage ended by death, not by choice or failure; she
is not looking, and the book should not stage her as available. What she is is *understood*,
professionally, for the first time in fifteen years, by a man who was in the room when she was
still going to be somebody.

## Narrative mode

**This spine is a guide to the text, not a prescription for it.** Where the manuscript and the
outline disagree, the manuscript is right and the outline gets updated. Scene sketches propose
a function and a set of materials; they do not dictate voice, tense, order of beats, or which
details survive. Anything here the drafting contradicts is superseded, not a constraint to be
honoured.

**Nālani's sections are first person, past tense** — established by the drafted S1, and the
reason "set for three" can carry an interior meditation on Ikaika away at school and on Tomás.
Daniel's sections are close third. Maya's, when Part Four opens her up, close third as well
unless the drafting says otherwise.

**S1 opens in present tense for two paragraphs and then settles into past. This is deliberate and is not to be normalized** (Ledger #26). Whether any later scene may open the same way is undecided.

The asymmetry does real work: she is the book's voice and he is its case study, which is the
right relation between the woman who understood it first and the man who signed the
cancellation. It also puts the reader inside the only person who is right, so the first act's
helplessness is a felt condition rather than an observed one.

Open: what voice the S26 coda takes, since it belongs to Lehua rather than to Nālani. First
person would pass on the inheritance of the voice along with the koa bowl; third would mark
that the person who saw all of it is gone.

Alongside both, the Fourteen Days found documents carry the planet while the scenes carry the
people. The reader sometimes meets the document first and the scene behind it a chapter
later — Bulletin 003's cancellation, then Daniel drafting his concurrence to it.

Part Four: time-jump chapters, years apart, documents continuing (IERS bulletins, insurance
circulars, hearing transcripts, the creep-rate chart). Each chapter is one scene on one day;
the jumps do the summarizing.

# PART ONE — THE DAY

### S1 · D−1, evening · Hilo · Nālani · chapter **Ice**, section **Hawaiʻi**

**Drafted — see `lyx/ice.lyx`. The manuscript supersedes this sketch.** An ordinary evening before any alert. Tūtū in her straight-back chair watching the sun go down, Lehua reading at the table, Nālani at the stove frying Spam musubi in a sugar glaze for the girl who won't speak to her, the glaze spitting and burning her hand. The first request spoken in the book is *get me ice*, and the daughter says she's reading. It closes on a table set for three, and on what three means: Ikaika away at school, Tomás six years gone.

**The opening runs present tense for two paragraphs and then settles into past. Deliberate — see Ledger #26.**

**The tales are staged as a catechism, and that is the scene's engine.** Nālani does not let Tūtū tell it. She quizzes Lehua, and Lehua has all of it — the mechanism, Waiākea by name, 1946, her great-grandmother's age, the reef, the fish standing on their sides — recited flat, in her mother's library-talk sentences. Tūtū supplies only the correction: 1960, sixty dead, the store, the streets gone forever. Memory in this house is a drill with right answers, which is the book's argument about Hilo made once, early, in a kitchen, and not made again until the water proves it.

**Lehua's silence is directed.** She refuses her mother ice and hands her mother the entire inheritance on demand. Her last line — *"Mom. Is that why you do the library thing?"* — **is never answered.** The phone takes it. See Ledger #22.

**Bzzzz.** The book's first section break is a phone. **Oscar**, her LLM assistant, set to watch hydroacoustic feeds for ice years ago when her grant proposals stopped being funded; four alerts in six years. Many watch those feeds; few watch for ice. She sends Lehua to clean up, meets Tūtū's steady gaze, goes to her room and **closes the door.**

**The investigation is typed, not spoken** — a monospaced command line on the page, which keeps Oscar an instrument and marks the shift out of the kitchen visually. HA01 Cape Leeuwin, 04:12:07Z: the cryogenic descending sweep, 11 Hz down to 4 Hz across 340 seconds. Then the amplitude, three orders of magnitude too large, and **she rejects the signal because it is too large** — then proves herself right, because HA01's current trace is railed, a dead hydrophone. Then Crozet (HA04) has the same waveform, appropriately late and not railed, and takes the rightness away from her. ΔT across two stations gives a hyperboloid, not a fix; its surface intersection is a hyperbola through Prydz Bay, origin 03:20Z, hedged as *if that was the source*. Oscar reports nothing out of Prydz Bay since 03:20 — Davis at 03:19, Zhongshan, Progress and Bharati at 03:20 — and she dismisses it flatly, because Antarctic telemetry drops. **She does not register that four independent stations stopped at the origin instant.** See Ledger #21.

She returns to the common area. The table is cleared, the light is gone. **Tūtū asks the only question she has ever needed — "How long."** Nālani gives her the textbook: it's ice, ice floats, it's already floating, it doesn't make a wave. Every word true, and identical to the desk consensus Daniel signs in S4. Tūtū turns back to the window.

*Function: expertise, community, and three generations of the same memory in one room — and the book's first believer reaching the same correct wrong answer as the institutions, several hours before they do.*

### S2 · D0, 7:00 am EDT · Silver Spring · Daniel
Breakfast: the Corolla cottage — repair estimate, flood insurance non-renewal, her refusal to
sell, his refusal to say what he thinks. Two people who like each other disagreeing about
money. Maya eats standing up because it is faster, not because there is anything to stay out
of range of. On her way out Ellen mentions a directorship that is opening — lightly, the fourth
time in ten years — and he says he'll look at it, and they both know he won't, and she kisses
him anyway.

Then the office, where four notices from four agencies sit in his queue and the desk consensus
assembles itself: floating ice, Larsen B precedent, displaces its own weight, no seismic source,
no wave. Every sentence true. He concurs.
*Function: plant the D+8 payoff; put Daniel on the record agreeing with the reasoning that
becomes Bulletin 003. His error is not negligence, and his marriage is not a wound. Both of
those are the point.*

### S3 · D0, mid-morning EDT · phone · both
**Not a cold call.** She has had his cell number for twenty years. That is the scene — an old
friend rung at 4 am his time by a woman who knew him when he was still going to be a scientist,
and who does not have to say *you reviewed my array proposal*, so does not, and it sits inside
the call the whole way through.

She makes the four-pins case: four sectors, one day, a circumpolar sequence — and then the
thing nobody has a desk for. The trough-mouth fans, loaded to the angle of repose, pore
pressure re-equilibrating on a clock of hours. **"I'm not calling about the ice. I'm calling
about the mud."** He gives her the textbook, correctly, in the voice he learned at Ewa Beach.
Somewhere in it he asks after the kids, and means it, and the question costs the call ninety
seconds it did not have.

After he hangs up he writes *pore pressure?* on a legal pad and does nothing with it, and the
doing-nothing is a scene, not a summary.
*Function: first contact, pre-loaded with two decades of history and one grievance. Her science
is right and unfundable; his caution is wrong and institutional. Neither is a villain.*

### S4 · D0, afternoon · Patrick SFB, then Silver Spring · document + Daniel
Sandoval's AFTAC screening note, presented as a document: signal non-nuclear, correctly
discriminated, no further action — the treaty machine working exactly as designed and therefore
telling no one. Then Daniel in the ops room as the Indian Ocean models converge on sub-decimetre
amplitudes and the cancellation goes out. He drafts the US concurrence himself. The chapter
ends on his signature.
*Function: every gate fails open and nobody makes a mistake. The reader watches the trap close
in real time.*

### S5 · D0, evening (rolling) · document interlude — the pulse
*Source: Timeline C-05.*
A weather-hobbyist forum thread, presented raw. A poster in Japan logs a clean 1.5 hPa blip;
then Perth, Réunion, Nairobi, the Azores — timestamps marching westward at 315 m/s, hobbyists
cross-posting home-station screenshots, someone starting a shared spreadsheet, nobody knowing
what it is a dataset *of*. One poster notes that Bharati would normally catch this and Bharati
is not posting. Thread locked by a moderator for speculation. Last visible post: *whatever it
was, it was everywhere.*
*Function: the planet hears it before the institutions understand it; plants the D+10 return.
The book's cheapest and largest scene.*

# PART TWO — THE MUD

### S6 · D0, night EDT · Silver Spring ops floor · Daniel
The Prydz fan lets go eleven hours after the ice. Daniel is in the building on an unrelated
pretext when the Arabian Sea gauges start posting. Jamnagar. Karachi. The room does not erupt;
it goes quiet, which is worse. Someone prints his concurrence memo without being asked to, and
he understands it has become an exhibit.

He calls the only person who was right, and the call is harder than a cold one would have been,
because he has to be wrong in front of somebody who watched him choose this life. It is
dinnertime in Hilo. She answers on the first ring, and she does not say I told you so, because
she is already on the next problem.
*Function: the false all-clear detonates. Their working alliance begins in the worst hour of
his career.*

### S7 · D0, ~8 pm HST · UH Hilo lab · Nālani
The whiteboard. Nālani, Keala, and one other undergrad run the circumpolar arithmetic and the
pore-pressure clocks. **The model she runs is Tomás's** — his code, his comments, his variable
names, a header block with his initials and a date from before the children could read. Nobody
in the scene remarks on it. Keala notices the initials and does not ask.

She writes the failure windows for the Crary, Ross and Belgica fans on **one sheet of paper** —
predicted 6.6 / 7.5 / 4.6 h — photographs it, and sends it to Daniel's personal phone before
the second fan lets go. Observed: 6.75 / 6.4 / 5.0. The sheet is time-stamped by the send.
Nobody in the scene says the word credential.
*Function: her authority earned on the page, in advance, with a document the book can produce
again at S21. Keala's first scene. And the quietest statement of what the Hilo decision cost:
her husband's last useful act is a subroutine.*

### S8 · D0, 9 pm HST → D1 pre-dawn · Hilo · Nālani
The evacuation, and it is a household before it is a coastline. Kawika Pang takes her call at
9 pm because it is her; the routes she has walked with library audiences for eleven years hold;
PTWC gets its warning from a woman its parent agency declined to fund.

Then her own three. Ikaika on the phone from the mainland, four hours behind understanding and
unable to get home, kept on speaker on the kitchen counter while they pack. Lehua carrying her
great-grandmother's bag and being sixteen about it. **Tūtū Ruth directs the packing** —
photographs, the koa bowl, nothing else — and Nālani, who is running a county's evacuation from
a cell phone, does what her grandmother says without arguing. *"We've done this."* Lehua has
not. Ikaika hears it happen through a phone.
*Function: the community work pays off as infrastructure; memory is the only warning system
that fires; and the woman coordinating it is also somebody's granddaughter and somebody's
mother in the same forty minutes.*

### S9 · D1, 2:40 am HST · Hilo · Nālani
The harbor event, written to Hawaii.md: the bay finds its note and rings — not one wave but a
seiche, building for hours, the harbor destroying itself in the dark by increments. The water
crosses the breakwater, takes the port, floods the bayfront parkland, and stops, roughly, at
the grass line where the town already retreated.

Dawn: Nālani stands at the edge of the flooded park on the spot where the family store stood,
ocean on one side, living town on the other. Lehua is with her, and Tūtū Ruth is in the car
because she has seen it twice and does not need to see it again. Casualties in single digits.
The harbor is gone; Hilo is not.
*Function: the book's moral center. One American town treated memory as policy, sixty years
early, and the water honored the treaty.*

### S10 · D1, ~4 pm UTC · document interlude — Lisbon
*Source: Sequence.md.*
One page. A Lisbon civil-protection communiqué, or a wire report from the Praça do Comércio —
the square rebuilt after 1755 taking water again, 271 years later, from a cause no one in 1755
could have conceived. A single line invoking Voltaire, or pointedly refusing to.
*Function: the world-historical register, bought for a page, placed so the Atlantic arrival is
already dread by the time Daniel's afternoon starts.*

### S11 · D1, ~1 pm EDT · Washington / Annapolis · Daniel
The Crary wave per EastCoast.md: two to four metres at the open coast, the Sandy comparison,
Norfolk flooded, the Chesapeake's bent shallow water stripping the wave before it reaches DC.
His own house is dry and he finds the dryness obscurely shameful. Maya is at a friend's in
Annapolis — real fear, brief peril, resolved in an hour; use it lightly.

**Ellen's first question, before Maya is confirmed safe: "What about Corolla?"** Play it as
what it is — not coldness, but a woman whose family has held one piece of ground for four
generations asking about it in the two seconds before she can ask about her daughter, and
hearing herself do it. The cottage takes water and stands. That is the trap: it stands.
*Function: the East Coast gets its inconclusive wave. The cottage surviving D1 makes D+8
personal rather than regional, and buys a week of false relief.*

# PART THREE — THE WEEK

### S12 · D+2–D+5 · Silver Spring / Hilo, by phone · both
Opens with the Bengal Fan (D+2–3) — the passing wave as a loading event, the mechanism named
aloud — then Norway on D+3, and the pattern stops being deniable. Daniel converts disgrace into
mandate: a fan-watch task force, Nālani as its scientific anchor, by phone, unpaid at first
because the paperwork moves slower than the mud. The 3 am calls acquire a rhythm. Somewhere
behind one of them the returning Lamb pulse crosses both their barometers (D-08), noted in a
sentence, planted at S5.

They talk pore pressure and then keep talking. Ellen notices the cadence of the calls rather
than their content and is **not jealous** — the marriage is good and she has met Nālani twice.
What she notices is that at 3 am her husband sounds like a man who wants something, and that in
twenty years she has never once heard him sound that way about a job she found him.
*Function: the pull begins, and it begins as the intimacy of being the only two people who
trust each other's numbers. See the triangle note and Dial 1.*

### S13 · D+4 · document — Sandoval, second appearance
Sandoval forwards the complete Day 0 SEL sequence to the new task force, unsolicited, with a
one-line cover: all four events, correctly discriminated, correctly filed. Handwritten
postscript — *"You'll want the rest of these. They were never wrong. Neither were we."*
*Function: the machine that was right gets its one face. The document is the scene.*

### S14 · D+5–D+7 · Silver Spring · Daniel
Makran (D+5, Mw 8.2) nearly kills the Currituck decision: Daniel's boss reads it as proof that
background seismicity is noise the task force is pattern-matching into signal. The attribution
argument — 1–10 kPa of tidal modulation against 0.6 kPa of new load — is had in front of him and
settles nothing, which is the point.

Then Currituck. Nālani's bottom-pressure argument; no agency owning the decision; **Daniel
forcing it** — the same gate that failed open on Day 0, now his to hold shut, in public, against
his boss's *premature*, with his name on it either way. A partial evacuation order goes out for
the Outer Banks and lower Chesapeake.

Ellen drives to Corolla anyway, to empty the cottage — **not in defiance of him.** She has spent
a week watching him be careful in public and does not experience her own husband as an authority
to be obeyed; she thinks there is time; he would have said there was time too, if she had asked.
She did not ask. That is the whole failure and it is nobody's fault. Maya goes with her. He
finds out from Maya's text.
*Function: his redemption arc and his marriage crisis are the same decision. He can warn a
coast and cannot make his wife treat him as a source.*

### S15 · D+8 · Currituck Banks / Silver Spring · both households
The slide goes mid-afternoon. Warning is hours from the precursor Nālani flagged, then minutes
once the margin fails — forty minutes from failure to shore. Ellen and Maya are on the causeway
inland with four generations of a family in the back seat, **because his evacuation order
emptied the roads a day early**: the order he could not make her believe is the reason she
lives. The cottage goes entirely — not flooded, gone, the lot itself resurveyed by the ocean.

That night, all three Griers in the Silver Spring kitchen. Grief for the house arriving as
grief for something neither of them will name yet.
*Dial staging: end on an action, not a verdict — Ellen washing the one dish, Daniel drying, Maya
watching from the doorway. Who leaves the room first is decided later. See Dial 2.*
*Function: D+8 lands inside the Washington household. The catastrophe and the marriage stop
being separate plots.*

### S16 · D+10 · document — the number
IERS Rapid Service bulletin: length of day +0.35 ms; polar motion displaced. Reproduced in full,
which takes a third of a page. Beneath it, a paragraph in Nālani's voice — an email to Keala's
cohort — saying that this is the only single number that expresses the whole thing, that it has
no consequence whatsoever, and that both halves of that sentence are true.
*Function: breath between the family's worst night and the hinge. Scale, without another wave.*

### S17 · D+12 · split scene, phone · both — PART THREE CODA
The hinge. Gauges are back in service and none of them return to predicted level. Nālani on the
lava shore at night, Daniel in the dark ops room, the same residual on both screens. One of them
says: **"It's not going back down."**

The disaster — the shape with an end on it — is over. The load has begun.
*Function: pivot from event to condition.*

# PART FOUR — THE LOAD

*Time-jump chapters, Decades.md throughout: the staircase (SL-02/03), the thresholds
(TH-01–08), the epistemic arc. Three POVs. Each chapter is one scene on one day.*

### S18 · M4 · Senate hearing room / video link · Daniel + Nālani
GRACE-FO's first monthly solution (E-01, the unarguable number), the altimetry step (E-06) and
the fingerprint map (E-07) reach the Hill in one briefing. The map puts the maximum on the North
Atlantic, and a senator says, slowly, *"You're telling me we got the most."*

Nālani, on the link, gives the fast-end rate and then concedes unprompted that her number sits

### S19 · Y2 · Hilo, county council chambers · Nālani
The harbor rebuild hearings. Money exists to rebuild at the old line; Nālani argues for a third
retreat and discovers that being right twice has made her locally unanswerable, which she
distrusts more than opposition. **Tūtū Ruth died that spring at 93**, the last of the 1946

*Function: Hilo does deliberately and in public what it did quietly in 1960 — the precedent the

### S20 · Y6 · a reinsurer's floor, Hartford / Silver Spring · Maya (POV debut) + Daniel

### S21 · Y9 · House committee room · Daniel; Maya in the gallery
The surge. The grounding line unpins and jumps — eighteen months at fifteen centimetres a year —

*Dial staging: whether Ellen is in the gallery beside Maya or at home is Dial 2's downstream

### S22 · Y14 · Norfolk · Maya
The dry abandonment, in a city that still works. Maya, 30, runs a federal buyout office and
walks a neighbourhood where the drains run backward on clear mornings (TH-03), the wellfield two
counties inland has gone brackish (TH-05), and the last insurer left four years ago (TH-01). The

*Function: Decades.md's governing idea staged — a place becoming uninhabitable while remaining

### S23 · Y15 · Honolulu / Hilo · Nālani + Marcus Yee
The Oahu chart. Nālani, 59, puts the Hilina GPS creep rate on one axis and the sea level curve

Keala, 35, runs the instrument side.

*Function: cock the gun on the page, with the decade of watching Hawaii.md recommends. The two
disputes get their scene and stay unresolved.*

### S24 · ~Y20 · the gun — **Dial 6**
Recommended version: **Hilina fires.** An ordinary rift eruption becomes the wedge; M7.9; no

Nālani, 64, spends the night at the civil defense center she has advised for thirty-five years,
and the morning explaining to national cameras that the flank pointing at the mainland — Nuuanu,
the northeast scar — has not moved, that nobody can say whether it will, and that both of those
sentences are the literature speaking.
*Function: pays the mechanism without a third continental catastrophe; returns the climax to
Hilo, where memory is the warning system; leaves the mainland gun cocked forever.*

### S25 · Y30 · a retreat authority field office, on the Chesapeake · Maya + Nālani
Managed retreat is a federal program with a logo, which is to say ordinary (SY-05). Maya, 46,

### S26 · Y60+ · Hilo, the third shoreline · Lehua — CODA
**Lehua Reyes, 76**, gives the annual tsunami-awareness talk at the Hilo library — the same

**There was never a morning when it had happened.**
*Function: the closing image given to the person who inherits both lines — the family's and the

## Dials

**1. The affair.** Options unchanged: (a) consummated during the fortnight; (b) unconsummated —
the pull on the page, acknowledged once, acted on never; (c) deferred to Part Four. **(b) still

a consummation in week two would need to be about appetite rather than need, which is a smaller
story. The strongest version keeps Ellen's grievance accurate and incomplete — the calls really
are just pore pressure, and they really are also not.

**2. The marriage.** Reframed. Nothing breaks a good marriage by attrition, so if it breaks it
breaks on the ambition question: Ellen finally understanding that he will never want what she
wants. That is a legitimate, non-villainous reason to leave a man you love. **Break points now
staged at S20 (notoriety costs her) and S21 (three offers, none taken)** rather than at the D+8
kitchen. If it holds, the same beats read as endurance and S25's absence line becomes about
distance rather than divorce.

**3. Ending point.** Decided: stabilization (S26). The novella exit (S9) and the hinge exit
(S17) remain marked if the manuscript ever needs to retreat.

**4. Sandoval.** Decided: two documents (S4, S13), no scenes.

**5. Names / cultural read.** Nālani's Native Hawaiian identity is worth keeping for the
store-in-the-parkland inheritance and worth a cultural read at manuscript stage. That read now
covers Lehua, Ikaika, Keala, Kawika, and the *tūtū* usage above. Reyes as the children's surname

**6. The gun.** (a) **Hilina fires ~Y20, beams Polynesia, mainland gun stays cocked** —

the payoff. If (b), S24–S25 need rebuilding and Maya's office moves west.

a specific, nameable failure in their history that neither has raised in six years, and gives S3
